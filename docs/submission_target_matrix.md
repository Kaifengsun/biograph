# PharmGraphRAG Submission Target Matrix

## Purpose

This document tracks publication targets for the PharmGraphRAG paper. It is a working shortlist, not a final venue decision. Every journal or conference must be rechecked immediately before submission because indexing status, journal metrics, article processing charges, and review timelines can change.

## Selection Rules

- Prefer credible Scopus-indexed journals with transparent editorial policies and a clear topic fit.
- Treat Scopus indexing as an eligibility threshold, not a quality guarantee.
- Verify Scopus status through the official Scopus Sources interface or the official downloadable source list before submission.
- Verify conference indexing through official Engineering Village, CPCI, Scopus, or prior proceedings records. Do not rely on organizer advertising alone.
- Avoid simultaneous submission of the same full manuscript to multiple venues.
- Preserve a journal-first route while maintaining a conference backup route for October 2026 application timing.

## Current Recommendation

The first journal to evaluate seriously is **Supply Chain Analytics** because its official aims and scope explicitly cover pharmaceutical and healthcare supply chains, resilience, compliance, AI, simulation, predictive analytics, and prescriptive analytics. It is not automatically the final target: the final choice depends on the strength of the repaired experiments, APC constraints, and advisor feedback.

The paper should also preserve a stretch route to **Expert Systems with Applications** if the retrieval evaluation, leakage-free simulation, and system contribution ablations become strong enough.
