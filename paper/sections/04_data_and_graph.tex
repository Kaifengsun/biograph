\section{Corpus and Evidence Graph}
\label{sec:data}

The frozen corpus contains 32 English regulatory and technical documents from ICH, FDA, EMA, and WHO sources. PDF-to-Markdown conversion and structural parsing produced 2,478 non-empty source chunks and 312 table records. \Cref{tab:corpus} summarizes the resources.

\input{tables/corpus_statistics}

The parser retained heading levels, parent-child links, and local order. Source-constrained enrichment added 3,917 generated question profiles, 221 section summaries, 32 document summaries, and 311 table summaries. Every generated item points back to a source unit and is excluded from Gold evidence.

Structured records came from an openFDA snapshot frozen on 10 July 2026 \cite{openfdaShortages}. The pipeline normalized names, package NDCs, availability, reasons, status, and dates into event, product, ingredient, and manufacturer nodes. Each record retains its raw-source pointer.

The graph contains 7,578 nodes and 15,237 edges. Supply edges connect events to NDC products, ingredients, and reporting companies; document edges encode containment, order, references, and supersession. The schema distinguishes alias mentions from causal claims and marks uncertain identity links as candidates rather than forced merges.
